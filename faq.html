FAQ

Clear answers before you submit an inquiry.

Common questions about timelines, revisions, hosting, ownership, maintenance, and payment methods.

How long does development take?

Portfolio and business websites typically start at 7 days. E-commerce websites typically start at 14 days. Final timing depends on scope, content readiness, revisions, and any add-ons.

Do I need hosting?

Yes. A website needs hosting to be available online. Velora Studio provides setup guidance, but clients own and purchase their hosting.

Do you provide domains?

Clients own and purchase their domains. Velora Studio can guide the setup process so the domain connects properly to the website.

Can I update my website?

Yes. Depending on your setup, you can update content yourself or request updates through a maintenance plan.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes. Basic maintenance starts at ₱1,500/month and includes text updates, image updates, monthly backup, and minor fixes. Standard and e-commerce plans are also available.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Maya Business for local clients and Wise for international payments. A 50% down payment is required to start, and the remaining 50% is due upon completion.

How many revisions are included?

Portfolio websites include 1 revision. Business and e-commerce websites include 2 revisions. Additional revisions can be added for ₱300 each.
